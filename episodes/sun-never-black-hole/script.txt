// ACT 1 — THE HOOK
Every star, sooner or later, runs out of fuel. Even our Sun. But here's what nobody tells you: it will never become a black hole. Not in this life. [src: nasa]

// ACT 2 — THE BAR
A black hole needs an entire star to collapse under its own gravity — at least two to three solar masses. The Sun has barely one. [src: kb, nasa]

// ACT 3 — THE RED GIANT
In five billion years the hydrogen runs out, the Sun swells into a red giant — swallowing Mercury and Venus — then sheds its outer layers in an expanding cloud of gas. [src: nasa]

// ACT 4 — THE WHITE DWARF
What remains is a white dwarf — an Earth-sized ember of leftover carbon, fading slowly. It was never massive enough. It never will be. The Sun ends as a whisper, not a scream. [src: nasa, kb]
